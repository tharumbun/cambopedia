A visual journey to one of Cambodia’s most majestic and remote heritage sites. Perched atop a cliff on the Dangrek Mountains, Preah Vihear Temple offers sweeping views and timeless beauty. This photo series captures the dramatic interplay of ancient stone carvings, shifting light, and the lives of people who dwell in the shadow of history—on the edge of a kingdom, where Cambodia meets the sky.
