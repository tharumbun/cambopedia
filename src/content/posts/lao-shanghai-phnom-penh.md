Lao Shanghai, located at 177 Preah Trasak Paem St. (63) in Phnom Penh, is a culinary gem that offers an authentic fusion of Lao and Shanghainese cuisines. Open daily from 7:00 am to 9:00 pm, this restaurant has become a favorite among locals and tourists alike.

Ambiance

The restaurant boasts a cozy and inviting atmosphere, blending traditional Lao and Chinese décor elements. The warm lighting and tasteful furnishings create a comfortable dining environment suitable for both casual meals and special occasions.

Cuisine

Lao Shanghai's menu is a delightful fusion of Lao and Shanghainese dishes, offering a unique gastronomic experience. Signature dishes include the Lao-style larb salad, known for its fresh herbs and bold flavors, and the Shanghainese xiaolongbao, delicate soup dumplings that burst with savory broth. The restaurant also offers a variety of vegetarian options, ensuring that all guests can find something to enjoy.

Service

The staff at Lao Shanghai are known for their attentive and friendly service. They are well-versed in the menu and are happy to provide recommendations based on individual preferences. The prompt service ensures that diners have a pleasant and seamless dining experience.

Pricing

One of the standout features of Lao Shanghai is its affordability. Despite the high quality of the food and service, the prices are reasonable, making it accessible to a wide range of patrons. This excellent value for money has been highlighted in various reviews and is a significant draw for the restaurant.

Conclusion

Lao Shanghai successfully marries the rich culinary traditions of Laos and Shanghai, offering a unique dining experience in the heart of Phnom Penh. With its inviting ambiance, diverse menu, excellent service, and reasonable pricing, it is a must-visit for anyone looking to explore the flavors of these two vibrant cuisines.
