Le Castella Bakery, located in the bustling Tuol Kouk district of Phnom Penh, has garnered a reputation for offering some of the most delectable Taiwanese-style sponge cakes in the city. Known for their soft, fluffy, and jiggly texture, these cakes have become a favorite among both locals and expatriates seeking a delightful dessert experience.

### Ambiance and Service

Upon entering Le Castella Bakery, customers are greeted with a warm and inviting atmosphere. The interior is modest yet comfortable, providing a pleasant environment to select and enjoy their baked goods. The staff is often praised for their friendliness and attentiveness, ensuring that patrons feel welcomed and well-served. One reviewer noted the "friendly welcome with air conditioning," which adds to the overall comfort of the bakery.

### Signature Offerings

The bakery's standout products are its Taiwanese-style sponge cakes, celebrated for their light and airy consistency. These cakes are available in a variety of flavors, catering to diverse palates. The original flavor remains a classic favorite, with one customer rating it a perfect "10/10." Other popular options include rocky road and pandan, though opinions on these vary; the rocky road flavor received an "8/10," while the pandan was rated "4/10" by the same reviewer.

For cheese enthusiasts, the cheese sponge cake is a must-try. One patron described it as the "most delicious and softest jiggly cake in Phnom Penh," recommending it be enjoyed while still warm to fully appreciate the melting cheese interspersed within the cake.

### Quality and Taste

Le Castella's cakes are lauded for their exceptional texture and balanced sweetness. The sponges are incredibly soft and light, making them a delightful treat without being overly rich. As one satisfied customer shared, "Softest and lightest cake I’ve ever eaten." Another reviewer, who admitted not being a typical cake lover, was impressed by the "huge soft tender delicious" cake, highlighting its appeal even to those who usually abstain from pastries.

### Pricing

While the quality of the cakes is undisputed, some customers find the pricing to be on the higher side. A standard cake is priced at approximately $10, which some consider steep. However, many agree that the taste and quality justify the expense. As one patron mentioned, "The cake is amazing... it was almost $10 but I don't really mind spending $10 for it since it was so good."

### Location and Accessibility

Situated at 01M Street 516 in Khan Tuol Kouk, the bakery is conveniently located but can present some challenges regarding parking, especially during peak hours. The area is known to be crowded in the evenings, making it advisable to visit during off-peak times or consider using motorcycles or bicycles for easier access. One reviewer noted, "A bit hard to park your cars during rush hours... Better idea to use motorcycles or bikes."

### Customer Service

The majority of patrons commend the bakery for its excellent customer service. Staff members are described as "nice and friendly," contributing positively to the overall experience. However, there have been occasional concerns regarding customer service and issues with receiving correct change, as highlighted by a customer who experienced discrepancies in exchange calculations.

### Conclusion

Le Castella Bakery stands out as a premier destination for those seeking authentic Taiwanese sponge cakes in Phnom Penh. Despite minor concerns about pricing and parking, the bakery's commitment to quality, taste, and customer satisfaction makes it a worthwhile visit for cake enthusiasts and anyone looking to indulge in a delightful dessert experience.
