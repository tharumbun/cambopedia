Techo International Airport, Cambodia’s state-of-the-art aviation hub, is not just a gateway to Phnom Penh but also a convenient starting point to explore the surrounding areas. Whether you’re on a layover or have some extra time before your flight, there’s plenty to see and do near the airport. From cultural landmarks to serene natural spots, here are some of the top attractions and activities to enjoy nearby.

## Exploring the Surroundings of Techo International Airport: Things to Do Nearby

1\. AEON MALL Mean Chey

For a modern shopping and entertainment experience, Aeon Mall 3 is a must-visit destination near Techo International Airport. This newly opened mall features a wide range of retail stores, dining options, and recreational activities, including a cinema and family-friendly attractions. It’s an ideal spot to spend time before or after your flight.

2\. Choeung Ek Genocidal Center (Killing Fields) Located about 15 kilometers from the airport, this historical site is a sobering but essential visit for those who wish to understand Cambodia’s turbulent history. The center provides insight into the Khmer Rouge regime and honors the victims with a peaceful memorial stupa.

3\. Local Markets and Street Food The areas surrounding Techo International Airport are dotted with bustling local markets where you can immerse yourself in Cambodian culture. Try traditional Khmer street food like nom banh chok (Khmer noodles), lok lak (stir-fried beef), and kuy teav (noodle soup). These markets are perfect for grabbing a quick bite or picking up souvenirs.

4\. Tonle Bati A short trip from the airport, Tonle Bati is a tranquil lakeside retreat featuring ancient Angkorian temples like Ta Prohm and Yeay Peau. It’s a peaceful escape where you can explore historical sites and enjoy a relaxing afternoon by the water.

7\. Pagoda Visits The area near Techo International Airport is home to several beautiful pagodas where you can experience Cambodia’s spiritual heritage. These tranquil sites provide an opportunity for quiet reflection and photography amidst ornate Khmer architecture.

9\. Cambodia’s Countryside For a quick escape from the urban hustle, explore the picturesque countryside near the airport. You can take a bike ride or a guided tour to experience the serene landscapes, rice paddies, and traditional Cambodian villages.

Tips for Travelers: - Many of these attractions are within a 20–30 minute drive from the airport, making them ideal for short visits. - Arrange for transportation in advance, either through ride-hailing apps or private car services. - Keep an eye on your flight schedule and allow plenty of time to return to the airport for check-in.

Final Thoughts Techo International Airport’s location makes it a convenient starting point for exploring some of Phnom Penh’s hidden gems. Whether you’re interested in history, culture, or relaxation, the area around the airport offers something for every traveler. Next time you’re passing through, take the opportunity to discover these nearby attractions and make the most of your journey.
