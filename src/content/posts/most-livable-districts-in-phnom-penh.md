Phnom Penh has seen rapid development over the years, making several districts attractive for both locals and expats. The best district often depends on your lifestyle preferences—whether you value proximity to work, access to schools, nightlife, or a quieter residential vibe. Many expats prefer Chamkarmon and Toul Kork for convenience, while locals often find Sen Sok and Chroy Changvar appealing for their growing infrastructure and affordability.

Here are five of the most livable districts in the city:

Chamkarmon (BKK 1, BKK 2, BKK 3, and Tonle Bassac)

\- Why It's Livable: Known for its vibrant mix of modern conveniences and local culture, Chamkarmon is a hub for expats and locals who enjoy a cosmopolitan lifestyle. - Highlights: - Wide range of cafes, restaurants, and bars. - Upscale apartments and boutique hotels. - International schools and medical facilities. - Iconic landmarks like Independence Monument and Bassac Lane.

Daun Penh

\- Why It's Livable: The historic heart of Phnom Penh, Daun Penh offers a mix of colonial architecture and modern living. - Highlights: - Close to key landmarks like the Royal Palace, National Museum, and Wat Phnom. - Riverside area (Sisowath Quay) with its lively dining and entertainment options. - Vibrant markets like Central Market and Kandal Market. - Cultural attractions and coworking spaces.

Sen Sok

\- Why It's Livable: A rapidly developing district with more affordable housing options compared to central areas. - Highlights: - Modern shopping malls like AEON Mall 2 and Makro. - Family-friendly neighborhoods with parks and recreational facilities. - Increasing number of international schools. - A quieter, suburban vibe while still being connected to the city.

Toul Kork (TK)

\- Why It's Livable: Popular with families and professionals, Toul Kork is known for its spacious residences and community vibe. - Highlights: - Tree-lined streets and less congested roads. - Shopping malls like TK Avenue. - A mix of international and local dining options. - Schools and universities, making it ideal for families.

Chroy Changvar

\- Why It's Livable: Located across the Tonle Sap River, Chroy Changvar offers a tranquil environment and stunning river views. - Highlights: - Gated communities and modern condos. - Recreational spots like Sokha Beach and Garden City Golf Club. - Proximity to the upcoming satellite city developments. - Easy access to the central city via the Chroy Changvar Bridge.
