TOB at GIA, a subsidiary of the renowned Cuisine Wat Damnak, has established itself as a notable dining destination in Phnom Penh. Located in the GIA Tower on St. Sopheak Mongkol Road, Sangkat Tonle Basak, Khan Chamkar Mon, this restaurant offers a casual dining experience that seamlessly blends traditional Cambodian flavors with contemporary culinary techniques.

Ambiance and Setting

Upon entering TOB at GIA, guests are greeted by a modern and inviting atmosphere. The interior design features sleek lines complemented by warm lighting, creating a comfortable environment suitable for both intimate dinners and group gatherings. The spacious layout ensures ample seating, while thoughtful decor elements reflect the rich cultural heritage of Cambodia, adding to the restaurant's charm.

Culinary Offerings

The menu at TOB at GIA is a testament to the restaurant's commitment to quality and innovation. Diners can expect a diverse selection of dishes that highlight the best of Cambodian cuisine, prepared with a modern twist. One standout dish is the mackerel salad paired with creamy avocado, a combination that has been praised for its freshness and harmonious blend of flavors. Another notable offering is the rice served with tender, melt-in-your-mouth duck, showcasing the kitchen's expertise in delivering comfort food with a gourmet touch.

For those seeking a hearty start to their day, the breakfast menu is available from 7:00 am to 10:30 am. Options include grilled pork ribs accompanied by rice, soup, and pickles, as well as duck and yellow noodle soup featuring liver dumplings. These dishes not only provide a satisfying meal but also offer a glimpse into the traditional breakfast fare of the region.

The beverage selection caters to a wide range of preferences. Coffee enthusiasts can indulge in classics like espresso, cappuccino, and latte, available in both hot and iced variations. For those with a penchant for something sweeter, options such as iced Milo and iced mocha are on offer, providing a delightful treat to complement the meal.

Service and Hospitality

The staff at TOB at GIA are known for their attentive and friendly service. From the moment guests arrive, they are made to feel welcome, with the team readily available to provide recommendations and accommodate special requests. The waitstaff's proficiency in English ensures clear communication, enhancing the overall dining experience for both locals and international visitors.


TOB at GIA successfully combines the rich culinary traditions of Cambodia with contemporary dining sensibilities. The thoughtfully curated menu, inviting ambiance, and exceptional service make it a standout choice for those looking to explore Cambodian cuisine in Phnom Penh. Whether you're a resident or a traveler seeking an authentic yet modern dining experience, TOB at GIA promises a meal that is both memorable and satisfying.
