How to Purchase IKEA Products in Cambodia

There are several ways to buy IKEA products if you’re based in Cambodia. Here are the most popular options:

1\. Order Directly from IKEA Online

IKEA’s official website allows international shipping in some regions, but unfortunately, Cambodia isn’t on their list. However, you can order from IKEA Thailand’s website and arrange for a courier or freight service to bring the items to Cambodia. This option may require a bit of logistical coordination and additional costs for shipping and customs clearance.

2\. Use a Freight Forwarding Service

Freight forwarding services are an excellent option for large orders. You can purchase your desired items from IKEA Thailand, send them to the forwarding service’s warehouse in Bangkok, and then have them delivered to Cambodia. While this approach is cost-effective for bulk purchases, it may not be the best solution for smaller orders due to higher shipping costs per item.
