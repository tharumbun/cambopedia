by Lyla Peyton and Lisa Ly

Jet Setting with Mama is a heartwarming and engaging travel memoir that captures the joys, challenges, and unforgettable moments of a mother-child adventure across three different countries. The author skillfully weaves personal anecdotes, cultural discoveries, and emotional insights into a narrative that feels both intimate and universal.

Each destination offers a unique backdrop for growth, bonding, and reflection, and the book beautifully balances vivid descriptions of landscapes and local customs with the emotional richness of traveling with a beloved parent. Whether navigating foreign streets, sharing local meals, or simply experiencing the wonder of the world together, the duo's journey is both inspiring and relatable.

What makes this book stand out is its focus on intergenerational travel. It’s not just about the places they visit, but how travel becomes a catalyst for deepening relationships and making lifelong memories. The writing is clear, warm, and occasionally humorous, making it accessible for a wide audience—from seasoned travelers to armchair dreamers.

In short, Jet Setting with Mama is a delightful reminder that the best travel companions are often the ones closest to home. Perfect for anyone who cherishes family, wanderlust, or both.
