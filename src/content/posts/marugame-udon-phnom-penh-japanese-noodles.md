If you're craving a comforting bowl of Japanese udon in Phnom Penh, Marugame Udon is the place to go. Located in the heart of the city, this casual eatery serves up freshly made udon noodles with a variety of flavorful broths, tempura sides, and rice bowls.

<img src="https://im.cambopedia.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/marugame_udon_phnom_penh.jpg" alt="Marugame Udon restaurant in Phnom Penh" loading="lazy" />



Authenticity and Freshness

What sets Marugame apart is its commitment to freshness. You can watch the udon being kneaded, cut, and boiled right in the open kitchen before it's served steaming hot. The texture is perfectly chewy, and the broths—from the classic Kake Udon to the rich Curry Udon—bring out deep umami flavors.

Must-Try Dishes

\- Beef Udon – A rich, savory broth with thinly sliced beef that melts in your mouth. - Spicy Miso Udon – A bold, flavorful choice for those who love a little kick in their soup. - Tempura Selection – Pair your noodles with crispy shrimp, vegetable, or chicken tempura for an extra treat.

Casual and Efficient Dining Experience

Marugame Udon follows a cafeteria-style service where you grab a tray, pick your udon, select your tempura, and pay at the counter. The setup makes it a quick and convenient choice, whether you’re grabbing a bite solo or dining with friends.

Final Verdict

With its affordable prices, fresh noodles, and authentic flavors, Marugame Udon is a must-visit for Japanese food lovers in Phnom Penh. Whether you’re in the mood for a light, warming bowl of soup or a hearty noodle meal, this spot delivers on taste and quality.
