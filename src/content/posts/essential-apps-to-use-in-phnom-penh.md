If you're in Phnom Penh, having the right apps can make your experience smoother, whether you're a local or a visitor. Here are five essential apps to have:

1\. PassApp (or Grab) – For Getting Around Why? Tuk-tuks and taxis are the most convenient way to get around Phnom Penh, and PassApp is the go-to for booking tuk-tuks at local rates. Grab also offers ride-hailing services, sometimes with better promotions.

2\. ABA Mobile – For Digital Payments & Banking Why? ABA Bank is widely used in Cambodia, and its app allows you to transfer money, pay bills, and even scan QR codes for cashless payments at restaurants, cafes, and shops.

3\. Tada – For Ride-Hailing Without Extra Fees Why? Unlike other ride-hailing apps, Tada doesn’t charge drivers commission, which sometimes leads to better fares. It’s a good alternative to PassApp and Grab.

4\. FoodPanda – For Food Delivery Why? Phnom Penh has a great food scene, and FoodPanda lets you order from hundreds of restaurants with home delivery.

5\. Khmer24 – For Buying & Selling Locally Why? Think of it as Cambodia’s version of Craigslist. You can find everything from second-hand motorbikes to apartments for rent.

Other useful apps: - Google Translate (with Khmer offline translation) - Cambodia Airports (for flight updates)
