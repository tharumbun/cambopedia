Phnom Penh is a vibrant and dynamic city that offers a fantastic shopping experience. Whether you’re on the hunt for electronics, gadgets, unique souvenirs, or everyday essentials, there’s something for everyone. With 2024 just around the corner, here’s an updated guide to help you get the best deals and make your shopping experience smooth and enjoyable.

Living or visiting Phnom Penh means enjoying an ever-growing array of products from around the world. Whether you’re browsing the colorful stalls of local markets or perusing high-end electronics in a sleek mall, the city’s vibrant shopping scene has you covered.

This guide ensures you’re equipped with the knowledge and tips to shop smartly, avoid pitfalls, and leave Phnom Penh with not just great buys but unforgettable memories. Love shopping in Phnom Penh? Here’s how to make the most of it.

Why Phnom Penh is a shopper’s paradise

Phnom Penh markets and shopping malls you should know

he Central Market (Phsar Thom Thmey) One of Phnom Penh’s most iconic landmarks, the Central Market remains a favorite for both locals and tourists. Renowned for its fresh seafood, it is the go-to spot for crabs, squids, and lobsters. The best time to shop is in the afternoon when prices drop slightly. Beyond seafood, the market offers an array of jewelry, clothes, and souvenirs. In 2024, the Central Market’s charm is further enhanced by improved facilities, making shopping more convenient for visitors.

Russian Market (Phsar Tuol Tumpoung) Popular among expatriates and tourists, the Russian Market is famous for affordable international brand clothing and shoes produced in Cambodia. It’s an excellent place to find bargains on jeans, t-shirts, and handcrafted souvenirs. The market also offers a delightful selection of Cambodian street food and snacks. Renovations in 2023 have added better lighting and ventilation, making it a more comfortable shopping experience.

Phnom Penh Night Market Located on Sisowath Quay by the riverside, the Phnom Penh Night Market is a vibrant spot for locals and visitors. It’s ideal for shopping for Cambodian-made crafts, clothes, and accessories. The market’s open-air food court continues to be a highlight, offering authentic Cambodian dishes accompanied by live music performances.

Thai Huot Market Thai Huot has grown significantly since its early days. As of 2024, it operates multiple locations, including Monivong Boulevard, Boeung Keng Kang, and Sen Sok. Known for its high-quality imported products, especially from the United States, it’s a favorite among expats seeking familiar brands. The market also features a robust selection of organic and health-conscious items.

Bayon Market Bayon Market continues to rival Thai Huot with its range of imported goods. With branches in central Phnom Penh and Tuol Kork, it’s a convenient choice for locals and expats. The market has expanded its product lines to include more eco-friendly and organic options in response to growing consumer demand.

Aeon Mall Phnom Penh now boasts three Aeon malls: 1. Aeon 1: The first and most centrally located mall on Sothearos Boulevard remains a hub for shoppers and diners. 2. Aeon 2: Opened in 2018 in Sen Sok District, this larger mall features more retail options, entertainment, and dining choices. 3. Aeon 3: Recently inaugurated in 2024, this newest addition in Chroy Changvar District offers high-end stores, a luxury cinema, and a rooftop garden. Aeon malls are popular for their modern amenities, international brand stores, and diverse food courts.

Olympia Mall Strategically located near the Olympic Stadium, Olympia Mall has grown into a bustling shopping destination. With over 150 stores as of 2024, it offers a mix of retail, dining, and entertainment options. Recent additions include a state-of-the-art fitness center and a luxury spa.

Chip Mong Noro Mall Located on Preah Norodom Boulevard, Chip Mong Noro Mall remains a favorite for its lifestyle and dining offerings. It’s part of the growing trend of compact community malls that cater to urban dwellers.

Fun Mall TK Phnom Penh’s post-pandemic retail landscape saw the rise of Fun Mall TK, a family-friendly destination in Tuol Kork. With dedicated play areas, kid-friendly stores, and weekend events, it’s perfect for parents looking for a fun outing with their children.

Makro Phnom Penh Makro continues to dominate the wholesale market scene, offering a wide selection of imported goods, especially from Thailand. Popular items include Thai Mama noodles and bulk snack packages. The bakery section is a favorite among shoppers, known for its fresh bread and pastries. The spacious layout makes it a convenient shopping destination for individuals and businesses alike.

TK Avenue Tuol Kork’s first open-air mall, TK Avenue, remains a popular choice for residents. Known for its boutique shops and fine dining options, it provides a more relaxed and upscale shopping environment compared to larger malls. Regular events and promotions keep it lively throughout the year.

Global House Cambodia Global House continues to serve as Phnom Penh’s one-stop shop for building materials, home appliances, and garden supplies. Located in Sen Sok, it’s an essential destination for homeowners and contractors. In 2024, the store expanded its product range to include more energy-efficient and sustainable options.

Exchange Square Exchange Square remains a premium shopping and office complex in the city center. The 19th-floor views are a standout feature, offering stunning perspectives of Phnom Penh’s skyline, including iconic landmarks like the Central Market and the riverside. Its luxury shops and fine dining options attract an upscale clientele.

Tips for a rewarding shopping experience in Phnom Penh

1\. Embrace Bargaining Culture Bargaining remains a vital part of shopping in Cambodia, particularly in local markets and smaller shops. Sellers often set an initial high price, so don’t hesitate to negotiate. A polite and friendly approach can go a long way in securing a great deal.

2\. Beware of Counterfeit Goods Phnom Penh is known for its bustling markets, but some may sell counterfeit items. Be cautious, especially when buying branded goods at extremely low prices. Stick to reputable shops and authorized dealers to ensure quality and authenticity.

3\. Inspect Quality and Authenticity For items like gemstones, textiles, and traditional handicrafts, inspect the products carefully. Research beforehand to understand what to look for in terms of quality. Knowledge is your best tool when shopping for unique items.

4\. Stay Alert to Pickpocketing Like any busy city, Phnom Penh has its share of opportunistic theft. Keep your valuables secure, carry bags in front of you, and avoid displaying expensive items in public to minimize risks.

5\. Carry Both Riel and Dollars While US dollars are widely accepted in Cambodia, having Cambodian Riel on hand is helpful for smaller transactions. Double-check your change to avoid receiving counterfeit notes.

6\. Shop Around Before Buying Prices can vary significantly from shop to shop, even for similar items. Take your time to explore different stores and compare prices to ensure you’re getting the best deal.

7\. Look for VAT Refunds If you’re planning substantial purchases, ask about Value Added Tax (VAT) refunds. Some stores participate in this scheme, allowing you to claim a portion of the taxes on eligible items when you leave Cambodia.

8\. Respect Local Customs Be culturally sensitive while shopping. Dress modestly and avoid aggressive haggling, which may come off as disrespectful. Treat vendors with kindness to create a positive experience for both parties.

Conclusion Phnom Penh’s shopping scene continues to evolve, blending traditional markets with modern malls. Whether you’re hunting for bargains, indulging in luxury, or exploring Cambodian crafts, the city offers something for everyone. With ongoing developments and new additions, 2024 is an exciting year to explore Phnom Penh’s retail and cultural landscape.
