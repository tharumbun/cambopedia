Few dining experiences are as satisfying as gathering around a bubbling pot of broth, dipping fresh ingredients, and sharing laughter with friends and family. Chinese hot pot has become increasingly popular in Phnom Penh, offering a rich variety of flavors, ingredients, and atmospheres. Whether you prefer the numbing spice of Sichuan mala or a light, herbal broth, the city has plenty of hot pot spots to satisfy your cravings. Here’s a roundup of some of the best places to enjoy Chinese hot pot in Phnom Penh.

A Review of the Best Chinese Hot Pot Restaurants in Phnom Penh

1\. Haidilao Hot Pot

Location: AEON Mall Phnom Penh & AEON Mall Sen Sok

Haidilao is a globally renowned hot pot chain famous for its exceptional customer service and high-quality ingredients. From the moment you walk in, you’re treated to a luxurious dining experience, with free snacks, manicures, and even performances from their signature noodle dancers. Their broth selections range from rich Sichuan mala to a nourishing mushroom and herbal soup. The quality of meat, seafood, and vegetables is top-notch, making it a favorite for both locals and expats. Be prepared for long wait times during peak hours, but the wait is worth it.

2\. Happy Lamb Hot Pot

Location: Russian Market Area

For those who enjoy a milder, more balanced hot pot experience, Happy Lamb Hot Pot is a fantastic option. This restaurant specializes in Mongolian-style hot pot, featuring a flavorful bone broth that enhances the natural taste of ingredients. Their lamb selections are particularly good, and they offer a variety of fresh vegetables and tofu options. It’s a great place for those who prefer a less spicy but equally delicious hot pot meal.

3\. Little Sheep Hot Pot

Location: BKK1

Little Sheep Hot Pot is another well-known chain that has made its way to Phnom Penh. Famous for its rich, aromatic broth that blends herbs and spices, this spot offers a comforting and flavorful hot pot experience. The quality of their meat, especially their lamb, is excellent. It’s a great place for those who enjoy a balanced mix of spices and flavors without the extreme heat of Sichuan-style broths.

Final Thoughts

Phnom Penh’s growing food scene has embraced Chinese hot pot, offering a range of experiences from budget-friendly casual spots to high-end, luxury dining. Whether you’re in the mood for the fiery kick of mala, the rich umami of bone broth, or the delicate flavors of Mongolian-style hot pot, the city has something for everyone.
