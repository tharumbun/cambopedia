Here's an overview of the available models and their key aspects:

Nothing Phone (3a) and Phone (3a) Pro

The latest additions to the lineup, the Phone (3a) and Phone (3a) Pro, offer well-rounded performance at an affordable price point. Both models feature a 6.77-inch AMOLED display with a 120Hz refresh rate, a 5,000mAh battery, Qualcomm Snapdragon 7s Gen 3 processor, 12 GB RAM, and 256 GB storage. The Pro variant includes an additional telephoto camera with 3X optical zoom. While the devices showcase a unique design and solid battery life, they lack an IP68 rating and wireless charging. The triple-camera system is versatile, though low-light performance and white balance could be improved. Notably, these models are currently available in the US through a beta program and may face connectivity issues with certain carriers.

Nothing Phone (2a) Plus

Released five months ago, the Phone (2a) Plus emphasizes minimalism, operating on Android with essential Google apps and minimal bloatware. It boasts a MediaTek Dimensity 7350 Pro 5G processor, a 50MP front camera, 50W fast charging, and a 6.7-inch AMOLED display. The design and functional Glyph lights stand out, making it a worthwhile option for new users, though current Phone (2a) owners might await a more significant update.
