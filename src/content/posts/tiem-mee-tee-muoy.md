Tiem Mee Ti Mouy: A Taste of Nostalgia and Comfort in Phnom Penh

If you’re on the hunt for a comforting bowl of noodles in Phnom Penh, Tiem Mee Ti Mouy (តៀមមីទីមួយ) should be on your list. This local favorite, whose name translates to “Tiem Mee Number One,” lives up to the title with its consistently flavorful offerings and nostalgic charm.

Old-School Vibes, Timeless Taste Stepping into Tiem Mee Ti Mouy is like walking into a scene from decades past. The décor is minimal, yet it echoes the classic noodle shops that many Phnom Penh locals grew up with. The space is clean and simple, with stainless steel tables, red stools, and a quiet hum of conversation – the kind of place that focuses on the food more than the frills.

The Star of the Show: Mee Kola and Dry Noodles Their dry noodles (មីស្ងួត) are the real highlight. Served with a delicate soy-based sauce, springy egg noodles, perfectly cooked pork slices, and a hint of garlic oil, each bite is layered with flavor. You can opt for soup on the side, which balances the dry noodles with a light, savory broth.

Another must-try is their Mee Kola, a regional noodle style that brings together sweet, sour, and salty in a delightful harmony. It’s rare to find a place in Phnom Penh that nails this dish so well.

Affordable and Fast Meals are reasonably priced, typically ranging between 7,000 to 12,000 riel (~$1.75 to $3), making it perfect for a weekday lunch or a weekend craving. Service is brisk but friendly – you order, and within minutes, your bowl is ready.

A Local Favorite for a Reason What makes Tiem Mee Ti Mouy stand out is its consistency. Whether you’re a regular or visiting for the first time, you’ll find the same taste and quality that keeps locals coming back. It's not trying to reinvent anything – just delivering traditional noodle dishes done right.

Final Verdict Best for: Noodle lovers, quick lunch, casual dining Don’t miss: Dry egg noodles with pork, Mee Kola, iced milk coffee

Whether you’re a Phnom Penh local or a visitor looking for authentic local eats, Tiem Mee Ti Mouy is a spot that warms both the stomach and the heart.
