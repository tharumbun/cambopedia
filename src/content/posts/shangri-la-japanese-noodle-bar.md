Shangri-La Japanese Noodle Bar in Phnom Penh is celebrated for its authentic Hokkaido-style ramen, offering a rich and flavorful broth that has garnered praise from both locals and visitors. The menu features a diverse selection of ramen dishes, including spicy variations, miso-based soups, and unique options like crab ramen. Beyond ramen, the restaurant offers a variety of Japanese dishes such as fried chicken bowls, curry bowls, and appetizers like takoyaki and dumplings. The establishment is noted for its quick service and inviting atmosphere, with seating options ranging from tables to small private dining areas and a bar. However, patrons should be aware that smoking is permitted inside the restaurant, which may affect the dining experience for some. For those preferring to dine at home, Shangri-La Japanese Noodle Bar offers delivery through platforms like Foodpanda, providing convenient access to their extensive menu.
