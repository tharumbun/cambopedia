Over the past few years, Phnom Penh has seen a surge in trendy cafés and dessert shops, catering to a growing audience of young Cambodians and expats looking for affordable yet tasty treats. Among them, Mixue Ice Cream & Tea has rapidly gained popularity, becoming a go-to spot for refreshing drinks and ice cream. The Chinese-born brand has expanded across Asia, and its entry into Cambodia has been met with enthusiasm, thanks to its budget-friendly menu, quality offerings, and vibrant aesthetic.

Mixue Ice Cream & Tea in Phnom Penh: A Refreshing and Affordable Treat

Affordable Indulgence

One of Mixue’s biggest selling points is its affordability. While many international dessert brands position themselves as premium, Mixue takes the opposite approach, offering high-quality ice cream and drinks at surprisingly low prices. This has made it an attractive option for students, office workers, and families looking for an inexpensive treat in Phnom Penh’s increasingly competitive café scene.

For as little as 3,000 to 5,000 riel (about $0.75 to $1.25), customers can enjoy a signature soft-serve ice cream or a refreshing fruit tea—a rarity in a city where similar items from other cafés can cost double or even triple the price. The affordability factor is undoubtedly a major driver behind Mixue’s rapid expansion and frequent customer visits.

Signature Menu Items

Mixue’s menu features a mix of milk teas, fruit teas, and ice cream-based desserts, with a few signature items that stand out:

Snow King Ice Cream – A rich, creamy soft-serve that has become an instant favorite. Its smooth texture and subtle sweetness make it a perfect dessert on its own or as a topping. Mango Pomelo Tea – A refreshing drink packed with fresh mango chunks and citrusy pomelo flavors, ideal for cooling down in Phnom Penh’s heat. Strawberry Lemonade – A balance of sweet and tangy flavors, this drink is both photogenic and thirst-quenching. Pearl Milk Tea – While Mixue’s boba isn’t as premium as those from dedicated bubble tea shops, it’s a great budget-friendly alternative for milk tea lovers.

Aesthetic and Atmosphere

Mixue locations in Phnom Penh follow the brand’s signature red-and-white color scheme, creating a playful and inviting atmosphere. The mascot, a cute snowman named “Snow King,” is prominently featured in store decorations and packaging, making the brand instantly recognizable. The shop interiors are designed for quick service rather than a sit-down café experience, but the bright and clean environment is appealing for short visits.

Unlike high-end coffee chains or independent artisan cafés, Mixue operates with a fast-food-style efficiency, meaning orders are prepared quickly. This makes it a convenient option for customers looking to grab something on the go. However, during peak hours, long lines can form, especially at high-traffic locations like Aeon Mall or near university campuses.

Final Verdict

Mixue Ice Cream & Tea has successfully carved a niche in Phnom Penh’s dessert scene by offering affordable, refreshing, and tasty treats. While it may not compete with premium ice cream or bubble tea brands in terms of complexity and richness, its value-for-money proposition makes it a standout choice. Whether you’re looking for a quick ice cream fix or a budget-friendly bubble tea, Mixue is worth a visit.
