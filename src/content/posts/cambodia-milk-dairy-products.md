Kirisu dairy is the only fresh milk produced by cows in Cambodia, — though it's noted that other farms (like Moo Moo and Angkormilk) also contribute to fresh milk production.

1. Kirisu Farm – Kirisu Dairy Products

Produced by Khmer Fresh Milk Co Ltd at Kirisu Farm in Takeo province, near Phnom Penh, this brand offers a wide range of fresh, farm-made dairy products:

Kirisu 100% Pure Milk

Kirisu 0% Fat Milk

Kirisu Barista Milk

Kirisu 100% Cream Milk

Kirisu All-Natural Yogurts (Plain, Strawberry, Vanilla, 0% Sugar)

Kirisu Chocolate and Strawberry Milk

2. Angkormilk – Liquid Milk, Yoghurt & Condensed Milk

Operated jointly by Vinamilk (Vietnam) and BPC Trading Co., Angkormilk runs Cambodia’s first milk manufacturing plant, launched in 2015. They produce:

UHT Liquid Milk (in small 220 ml pouches)

Yoghurts (four flavors)

Sweetened condensed milk under two brands: Best Cows and Captain

Also offer “Angkormilk Liquid Milk for Kids” (infused & pasteurised in small cartons)

3. Moo Moo Farms – Fresh Milk

Located in Kandal province, near Phnom Penh, Moo Moo Farms is a local fresh-milk producer. Although specific brand names aren't widely advertised, their farm produces between 1,300–1,500 litres of fresh milk daily 

4. Techo Sen Russey Treb Milk – Fresh Milk

This fresh-milk producer operates in Preah Vihear province, with a daily capacity of 600–700 litres
