Sakura Buffet BBQ & Soup has established itself as a notable dining destination in Phnom Penh, offering a unique blend of barbecue and soup buffet experiences. With multiple locations across the city, including branches at Rathana Plaza, Toul Kouk, and previously at Noro Mall, the restaurant caters to a diverse clientele seeking both variety and value.

Ambiance and Service

The Rathana Plaza branch is known for its spacious setting, making it ideal for gatherings with friends and family. However, during peak hours, especially after 6 PM, the restaurant tends to be packed, leading to potential delays in service. Some diners have noted that the smoke extraction from the BBQ could be improved to enhance the overall dining comfort.

Similarly, the Toul Kouk branch offers ample space suitable for large parties. While the food quality is generally praised, some patrons have reported slower service during busy periods and a limited dining time of two hours.

Culinary Offerings

Sakura Buffet BBQ & Soup provides an extensive selection of marinated meats, fresh vegetables, and flavorful broths, allowing diners to craft personalized dishes. The buffet setup caters to both BBQ enthusiasts and soup lovers, ensuring a versatile dining experience. Many guests appreciate the freshness of the ingredients and the tenderness of the meats.

Pricing

The restaurant offers a reasonable pricing structure, with adults enjoying the buffet at approximately $10, and children at half the price. This pricing includes a two-hour dining window, which most patrons find sufficient to savor the variety of offerings.

Conclusion

Sakura Buffet BBQ & Soup stands out as a commendable choice for those seeking a buffet-style BBQ and soup experience in Phnom Penh. While there are areas for improvement, particularly in service efficiency during peak times and enhancing the dining environment, the restaurant's diverse menu and reasonable pricing make it a worthwhile visit. Whether you're planning a casual meal with friends or a family gathering, Sakura Buffet BBQ & Soup offers a satisfying culinary journey that caters to various palates.
