**Cambodia on BBC’s 2026 Travel List**

Phnom Penh is featured on the BBC’s list of the 20 best places to travel in 2026, noted for cultural and creative developments in the city. 

The list itself includes a diverse set of global destinations, spanning cities, islands, and nature experiences. 

Why Cambodia Is Getting Attention

Even beyond BBC’s list:

Cambodia as a destination — especially Siem Reap with Angkor Wat — is widely recognized as one of Southeast Asia’s top travel highlights and appears on other 2026 travel trend lists (such as Lonely Planet’s “Best in Travel 2026”). 
Time Out Worldwide

The country’s tourism sector is actively being promoted internationally, including partnerships to raise its global profile. 
Travel And Tour World

Top Cambodian Highlights for Travelers

Whether or not you only base your decision on BBC’s list, Cambodia offers many iconic attractions travelers love:

Angkor Archaeological Park — UNESCO World Heritage site with Angkor Wat and dozens of temple complexes. 

Siem Reap city — lively hub for culture, markets, and local experiences. 

Phnom Penh — dynamic capital with museums, riverside boulevards, and growing creative scenes. 

Beaches & Islands — from Sihanoukville to Koh Rong. 

Countryside regions — like Kampot, Kep, Mondulkiri, and Battambang.
