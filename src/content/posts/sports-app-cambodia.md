## **Best Apps for Sports Enthusiasts in Cambodia**

Whether you're a weekend runner, a football fanatic, or just starting your fitness journey, having the right app can boost your performance and motivation. In Cambodia, the rise of health-conscious lifestyles has led to more people turning to digital tools to stay on track. Here are some of the best apps for sports and fitness enthusiasts in the Kingdom.

### 1. **Decathlon App**

The Decathlon app is a must-have for anyone looking to buy affordable and quality sports gear in Cambodia. It allows users to browse and order a wide range of products — from footballs to yoga mats — with convenient home delivery or store pickup from locations like Phnom Penh’s AEON Mall.
Download Decathlon app: [iPhone](https://apps.apple.com/sg/app/decathlon-shopping-app/id1494108710) & [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=sg.decathlon.app&hl=en_SG)

### 2. **Strava**

Popular among runners and cyclists, Strava helps track your workouts via GPS, share your progress with friends, and join challenges. Many Cambodian cyclists and runners are forming local clubs and using the app to organize weekend rides or runs.

### 3. **MyFitnessPal**

For those focused on overall fitness and nutrition, MyFitnessPal is an excellent calorie counter and food diary. It’s widely used by gym-goers in Cambodia who want to balance their diet with their workout goals.

### 4. **Nike Training Club**

Offering free workouts from strength training to yoga, this app is great for those working out at home or the gym. It’s especially popular with Cambodia’s growing fitness community who want guided routines without a personal trainer.

### 5. **YouTube**

While not a traditional fitness app, YouTube is the go-to platform for Cambodian sports lovers — from football highlights and Muay Thai fights to home workout tutorials and yoga classes.

**Bonus Tip:** Looking to join a sports community? Check Facebook for local groups like “Phnom Penh Runners” or “Cambodia Cycling Club” where members often share app tips and organize meetups.
