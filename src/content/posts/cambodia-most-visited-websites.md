Cambodia's digital landscape continues to grow rapidly, driven by a youthful and tech-savvy population. From social media platforms to e-commerce sites, the country's most visited websites reflect how Cambodians consume content, connect with others, and shop online.

Cambodia’s most visited websites offer a glimpse into the nation’s evolving digital habits. Social media dominates, with platforms like Facebook, TikTok, and YouTube taking the lead. At the same time, local players like Khmerload, Sabay, and Vtube show that there’s a strong appetite for culturally relevant content.

As Cambodia’s digital ecosystem grows, it’s exciting to see how these platforms shape the country’s online culture and business landscape. Whether you’re a marketer, entrepreneur, or curious observer, understanding these trends is key to navigating Cambodia’s digital frontier.

1\. Facebook Category: Social Media Facebook remains the dominant platform in Cambodia, serving as a hub for social interactions, news updates, and small business promotions. With over 11 million users, it’s where Cambodians connect with friends, share updates, and follow brands.

2\. YouTube Category: Video Streaming YouTube is the go-to platform for entertainment, education, and music. From trending Vloggers to the latest hit songs in Khmer, Cambodians turn to YouTube for a variety of content.

3\. Google Category: Search Engine Google is the backbone of Cambodia’s online search behavior, whether for learning, researching, or finding businesses. Its search engine and services like Google Maps are indispensable in daily life.

4\. TikTok Category: Social Media TikTok has exploded in popularity in Cambodia, especially among Gen Z. From dance challenges to comedic sketches and educational content, TikTok keeps the younger generation entertained and engaged.

5\. Khmerload Category: News & Entertainment As a local entertainment and news website, Khmerload delivers the latest celebrity gossip, lifestyle tips, and trending news. It’s one of Cambodia's most widely read digital publications.

6\. Vtube Category: Local Video Platform Vtube, Cambodia’s local video-sharing platform, is gaining traction as an alternative to global video giants. With a growing library of Khmer content, it appeals to those who prefer locally produced material.

7\. Amazon & Alibaba Category: E-Commerce While not locally based, international e-commerce platforms like Amazon and Alibaba are increasingly popular as Cambodians explore online shopping. Tech enthusiasts and business owners often use these platforms for purchasing goods not available locally.

8\. E-commerce Platforms (Smile Shop, Shop.com.kh) Category: Online Shopping Cambodia’s homegrown e-commerce platforms are thriving, with Smile Shop and Shop.com.kh leading the way. These platforms cater to the growing demand for online shopping, offering everything from electronics to daily essentials.
