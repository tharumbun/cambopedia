As of May 2025, Phnom Penh's skyline features several towering structures, reflecting the city's rapid urban development. These buildings highlight Phnom Penh's transformation into a modern urban center, with international investments contributing to its architectural growth.

## Here are the five tallest buildings in Phnom Penh:

### KBX Universal Financial Center

Height: 264.6 meters Floors: 63 Status: Topped out; expected completion in 2026 Investor: Taiwan Notes: Upon completion, it will become the tallest building in Phnom Penh.

### Morgan Enmaison 2

Height: 243.8 meters Floors: 53 Status: Completed in 2023 Investor: Hong Kong Notes: Currently the tallest completed building in Phnom Penh.

### Urban Village Sky Prime Tower

Height: 242.1 meters Floors: 63 Status: Completed in 2024 Investor: Taiwan Notes: A significant addition to Phnom Penh's skyline.

### The Peak Shangri-La Tower

Height: 236 meters Floors: 58 Status: Completed in 2022 Investor: Singapore Notes: Features a mix of residential units and the Shangri-La Hotel.

### The Peak Residential Towers 1 & 2

Height: 224 meters Floors: 2 x 55 Status: Completed in 2022 Investor: Singapore Notes: Twin residential towers complementing the Shangri-La Tower.
