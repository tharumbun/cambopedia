In a landmark collaboration that underscores the deepening cultural ties between Cambodia and China, the upcoming film The Angkor Code promises to be a thrilling adventure-action movie set against the majestic backdrop of Angkor Wat's ancient civilization. This co-production between China's Shinework Pictures and Cambodia's Soma Group was formalized with the signing of a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) in Phnom Penh on April 11, 2025.

## Unveiling 'The Angkor Code': A Cinematic Bridge Between Cambodia and China

### A Tale of Two Nations

The Angkor Code is envisioned as an epic journey that traverses both Chinese and Cambodian landscapes, weaving a narrative that celebrates the rich histories and cultures of both nations. The film aims to showcase Angkor Wat's historical heritage and modern vitality, bringing together top-tier creative teams from China, Cambodia, and the international film industry.

Krasna Cham, Chairwoman and CEO of Soma Group, emphasized the significance of this project, stating that it is a landmark event for cultural exchange between Cambodia and China. Similarly, Jonathan Shen, Chairman of Shinework Pictures, highlighted the collaboration as an effort to present Cambodia's contemporary spirit and the friendship between the two nations.

### A Visual and Intellectual Feast

With leading filmmakers from both countries collaborating, The Angkor Code promises to deliver a visual and intellectual feast that blends Eastern wisdom and spectacle for global audiences. The film will be primarily shot in both China and Cambodia, bringing to life the millennia-old civilization of Angkor Wat through a compelling narrative and stunning visuals.

### Strengthening Cultural Bonds

This collaboration is more than just a cinematic endeavor; it represents a strengthening of cultural bonds under initiatives like the Belt and Road Initiative. By co-producing films like The Angkor Code, both countries aim to promote mutual cultural appreciation and showcase their shared histories and values to the world.

As anticipation builds for The Angkor Code, audiences worldwide can look forward to a film that not only entertains but also educates and inspires, shedding light on the rich tapestry of history and culture that defines Cambodia and China.
