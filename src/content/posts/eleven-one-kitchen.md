Phnom Penh’s food scene is a mix of old and new, where street food vendors, fine dining restaurants, and trendy cafés coexist. Among these, Eleven One Kitchen stands out as a restaurant that blends sustainability, local flavors, and a cozy atmosphere. With two locations in the city—one in Boeung Keng Kang (BKK1) and the other in Russian Market—this eco-conscious eatery has become a favorite for both locals and expats seeking delicious Khmer and fusion dishes.

Eleven One Kitchen: A Taste of Sustainability and Khmer Comfort Food in Phnom Penh

Ambience and Atmosphere

Stepping into Eleven One Kitchen feels like entering a serene oasis away from the bustling streets of Phnom Penh. The restaurant's design incorporates plenty of greenery, wooden furnishings, and a laid-back, tropical aesthetic that immediately puts you at ease. The outdoor seating areas, particularly at the BKK1 branch, are shaded and offer a relaxed setting for casual meals or group gatherings. Inside, the space is well-lit, airy, and decorated with locally sourced materials, reinforcing the restaurant’s commitment to sustainability.

Sustainability Commitment

One of Eleven One Kitchen’s most distinguishing features is its dedication to sustainability. The restaurant prides itself on using organic ingredients whenever possible, sourcing locally grown produce, and eliminating plastic waste. Drinks are served with bamboo straws, and takeaway packaging is biodegradable. For eco-conscious diners, this commitment to the environment adds an extra layer of appeal to the dining experience.

Food and Menu Selection

Eleven One Kitchen specializes in Khmer cuisine with a modern twist. Their menu features a variety of traditional Cambodian dishes, alongside some Western and fusion options. Here are some standout dishes:

Khmer Classics

Fish Amok – A must-try when in Cambodia, Eleven One Kitchen’s Fish Amok is rich, creamy, and bursting with the aromatic flavors of coconut milk, lemongrass, and turmeric. The fish is perfectly cooked, tender, and infused with traditional Khmer spices.

Beef Lok Lak – This Cambodian staple is done exceptionally well here. The beef is tender and flavorful, served with a tangy dipping sauce and fresh vegetables. It pairs perfectly with a side of rice and a fried egg.

Kampot Pepper Chicken – This dish highlights Cambodia’s world-famous Kampot pepper. The chicken is stir-fried to perfection, with a fragrant, peppery kick that lingers pleasantly on the palate.

Vegetarian and Vegan Options

Tofu and Mushroom Amok – A great plant-based alternative to the traditional Fish Amok, this dish retains all the creamy, spiced flavors while using tofu and mushrooms as a hearty base.

Pumpkin Coconut Curry – A mildly spiced, comforting dish that’s both flavorful and healthy. The pumpkin is soft and velvety, complemented by the rich coconut broth.

Vegetable Stir-Fry with Cashew Nuts – A light, satisfying option with a great balance of textures and flavors.

Refreshing Drinks

Eleven One Kitchen’s drinks menu is just as impressive as its food selection. Their fresh fruit juices and smoothies are made without added sugar, letting the natural sweetness of the fruits shine. The lemongrass and ginger tea is a refreshing and soothing choice, while their iced Cambodian coffee provides the perfect caffeine kick to beat Phnom Penh’s heat.

Service and Pricing

Service at Eleven One Kitchen is consistently warm and friendly. The staff are attentive and happy to offer recommendations if you’re unsure about what to order. While not the cheapest option in Phnom Penh, the pricing is reasonable considering the quality of ingredients and the portion sizes. Most main dishes range between $4 to $7, making it an affordable choice for those looking for high-quality, responsibly sourced food.

Final Verdict

Eleven One Kitchen is more than just a restaurant—it’s a reflection of Cambodia’s evolving food scene, where sustainability meets tradition. Whether you’re craving a classic Khmer dish, looking for a healthy vegetarian meal, or simply want to enjoy a relaxing dining experience, this place has something for everyone.

For expats, tourists, and locals alike, Eleven One Kitchen offers a guilt-free dining experience that satisfies both the stomach and the conscience. If you’re in Phnom Penh, this is definitely a spot worth adding to your food itinerary.
